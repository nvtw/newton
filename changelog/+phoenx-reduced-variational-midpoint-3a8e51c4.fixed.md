Fix energy gain and loss in reduced-coordinate `SolverPhoenX` articulations whose links spin fast, such as a spinning rope landing on rough ground: a tree whose links' gyroscopic motion would make the explicit step gain more than 1% of its energy per second now takes a symmetric variational midpoint step that conserves its momentum, its energy, and each link's spin about joint-free axes, instead of the dissipative linearized implicit step.
