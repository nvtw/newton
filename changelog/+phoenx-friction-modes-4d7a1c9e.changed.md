Apply each shape's ``ShapeConfig.friction_mode`` in ``SolverPhoenX``: a contact enables the union of its shapes' sliding, torsional, and rolling friction and is frictionless only when both shapes, or the rigid shape of a particle or cloth contact, are ``NONE``. Torsional and rolling friction now need ``ShapeFrictionMode.SLIDING_TORSIONAL``, ``SLIDING_ROLLING``, or ``SLIDING_TORSIONAL_ROLLING``; set one of them on the shapes that should keep the previous behavior, because the default ``SLIDING`` mode no longer applies ``mu_torsional`` or ``mu_rolling``.
