Add experimental `CollisionPipeline(ccd=True)` for continuous collision detection: rigid contacts that the current velocities can close before the next `collide()` call are kept for all bodies, including articulation links, so with `SolverMuJoCo` (Newton contacts) and `SolverKamino` fast bodies no longer tunnel through thin geometry or each other. `SolverXPBD`, `SolverSemiImplicit` and `SolverFeatherstone` warn that they do not enforce these contacts.
