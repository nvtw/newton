`SolverMuJoCo` now enforces speculative contacts from a `CollisionPipeline` created with `speculative_contact_gap_max`: a separated contact activates only in the substep in which the bodies would close the gap, and then stops them at the surface instead of after they penetrate or tunnel. Contacts whose shapes do not touch within the substep, for example when a body passes beside an edge, no longer push along their stale normal. Contacts from pipelines without speculative contacts behave as before.
