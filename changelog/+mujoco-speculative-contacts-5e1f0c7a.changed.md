`SolverMuJoCo` now honors speculative contacts from a `CollisionPipeline` created with `speculative_contact_gap_max` or `ccd=True`: a body that would pass a quarter of the thinner solid's thickness within one substep is bounded there, so fast bodies no longer tunnel through thin geometry, while ordinary impacts keep MuJoCo's compliant response. Contacts whose shapes do not touch within the substep, for example when a body passes beside an edge, no longer push along their stale normal. Contacts from pipelines without speculative contacts behave as before.
