  • Three vec2 planes: padding-free, but end-to-end remained about 3.5% below the adjacent baseline.
  • Six scalar planes with raw-pointer access: regressed to 1.81M steps/s. Perfect coalescing did not repay six distant streams and dependency latency.
  • Explicit consecutive-parent register reuse also regressed the row builder 0.3% and was removed.
  • All rejected Newton solver experiments are removed.

Warp array_noalias verdict

The feature is correct and now propagates through nested wp.func calls. It removes redundant loads in its PTX regression. On real G1 physics:

  • Long end-to-end brackets: about 1.0% mean gain.

Keep the Warp changes only as an uncommitted research branch; current evidence is not large enough to recommend merging. General forced vec4 widening was below 1% in a synthetic oracle and has been removed from Warp.
