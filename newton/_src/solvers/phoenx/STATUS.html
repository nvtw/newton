Matched production measurements put PhoenX at about 89% of nanoG1 end-to-end throughput. Phase isolation now shows that physics rollout is close; the remaining wall-clock gap is mainly in the PPO update.

Corrected diagnosis

An experimental overlap benchmark had inherited non-production environment defaults. It now uses the production G1 recipe and update-first launch order, with a regression test. Corrected isolation measures about 276 ms per PhoenX rollout versus roughly 270 ms for nanoG1, but about 250 ms per PhoenX update versus roughly 94 ms for nanoG1.

Retained wins

  • Compact reduced-factor storage and DOF-type factor split: less memory and up to +2.8% isolated physics.
  • Packed contact gather and selective launch tuning: substantial isolated physics wins, about +1% training where measured.
  • Dense learner tiling and tiled Muon: about +6% and +3.3% G1 training respectively.
  • Reusable contact graphs and topology caching: generic gains across homogeneous robot fleets.
  • Ant PPO work restored reliable learning; the current smoke remains finite near 0.93M samples/s.

What limits us now

  • CUDA-graph overlap hides about 43% of the PhoenX update behind physics, but the exposed learner tail sets the remaining gap.
  • Dense forward/weight-gradient passes, MinGRU, and Muon Newton-Schulz matrix work dominate the learner.
  • nanoG1/PufferLib keeps persistent BF16 parameter shadows and uses tensor-core GEMMs, including batched equal-shaped Muon matrices. PhoenX currently repeats casts and runs more separate Warp matrix passes.
  • Next: prototype general persistent BF16 shadows and BF16/batched Muon, then gate on G1, Ant/shared PPO, numerical checks, and learning.

Correctness guardrails

  • FP16 contact rows were removed earlier: their roughly 1% gain did not justify the complexity. Current production rows are FP32.
  • Commit e549a1ea2 fixes mixed-material grouping and per-contact friction scaling with end-to-end regressions.
  • Learning comparisons require multiple seeds and a frozen quality gate; one fast seed or checkpoint is not proof.
